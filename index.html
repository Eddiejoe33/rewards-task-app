ZapChurch

Church App Product Preview

ZapChurch is a church engagement and discipleship system. Members grow through structured faith paths, complete commitment-based tasks, receive real church-approved rewards, and unlock access to service and leadership opportunities.

Faithfulness — not attendance alone — unlocks recognition, rewards, and responsibility.

This feature is active in the full ZapChurch app. Churches can customize tasks, rewards, and approvals.
